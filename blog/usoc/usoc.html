New York Pancyprian Freedoms

The Pancyprian Freedoms are a holdover from a traditional of ethic teams that goes all the way back to expat teams like Maccabi Los Angeles, the Philadelphia Ukranians, and the Greek Americans. Following Turkey's 1974 invasion of Cyprus, the Pancyprian Freedoms were founded in an effort to provide opportunity to Greek and Cypriot athletes. They went on to be enormously successful in the USOC, willing 3 times in the pre-MLS era. They've also recently gone on a Cinderella run, having beating FC Cincinnati II last year on penaltiesbefore falling to Tyrod Taylor's Westchester SC.

The Freedoms play in Belson Stadium at St. John's University. It's small, and I've seen a game there before !

New York Greek Americans

Next are the Pancyprian Freedoms hated hellenic rival, playing at the (reportedly very nice) Met Oval in Maspeth, Queens are the Greek Americans. Founded in 1946, they've won the USOC four times, in 1967, 1968, 1969 and 1974, making it the (joint) 2nd most successful club in the competition's history. I should mention that the prize for winning the Open Cup is a spot in the CONCACAF Champions League, an international competition between North American teams. So they have actually played (and claim to have beaten!) teams in Mexico.

They also have a cigar night!

Red Hook FC

Now, in contrast to the 80 year old Greek Americans, is Red Hook FC. Founded in 2024, as an amateur side, Red Hook immediately went on to win back-to-back promotions (the amateur leagues have that here!) to the UPSL premier, a top semi pro league!

Aside from the fantastic football manager-esque storyline, RHFC appears to also have a bit of a burgeoning fanbase, going off of pictures from their website.

But honestly, I am biased. I would absolutely love to take the free IKEA ferry to Red Hook to watch a free USOC game. That sounds amazing.

New Amsterdam FC

On a confusing and dour note, New Amsterdam FC! New Amsterdam FC was a project of Laurence Girard to bring a NISA team to the big apple!

But where would Girard put the team? Lower division soccer teams often play at high schools or colleges, like Fordham, St John's, Hofstra. There's also the historic if inaccessible Icahn stadium, the Met Oval is nice...

Warwick New York! Although, to be fair, this was during COVID times.

Head coach Eric Wynalda would resign just 4 days before the inaugural season. Girard was subbed on as a goalkeeper in the second half of a game against the (then) New York Cosmos. He is the owner of the team. Girard went on to make coaching hires based on who would allow him to play in goal.

But next season was better than their inaugural COVID year. They moved to Hempstead (old home of the Cosmos, on Long Island not really NYC) and essentially ran out of money and folded. However, not before the owner had a meltdown and began buying fans pizza.

I should mention at the end that there are some unsavory things out there about Girard. So while the NAFC story is really something, I can't say I'm rooting for them.

I had been confused to even see NAFC listed in this year's USOC, however, now I understand why! Welcome back Mr. Girard!

Lansdowne Yonkers FC

Lansdowne Yonkers, previously known as the much cooler Lansdowne Bhoys, play in the Cosmopolitan Soccer League, same as the Freedoms and the Greek Americans. They are notable for a special affiliate relationship they have with Celtic, which dovetails nicely with the teams heritage. In 2022 they lost 1-0 to Flower City Union in Rochester NY. Flower City Union went on to play DC United in that run!

New York Athletic CLub

NYAC is interesting. To start, Western soccer teams love to be called "AC" because it sounds fancy and it's kind of obscure. However, if you just play soccer, you're a soccer club ? So often times it's just a nominal facade that sounds kind of interesting.

Not NYAC. NYAC is basically a gym. You could get a membership there! It would only cost you an initiation fee in the 10k range and 4k in annual dues, plus other fees. Also you can't get a membership there, not without a referral from an existing member, as well as approval from the club itself.

So, I'm not sure if the players are all 10k in the hole, but the club does happen to feature a pretty consistently good soccer team, given they've regularly qualifying for the USOC.

On their website, they claim "The organization based on three pillars of achievement: ability, reliability and character." which is not proper grammar.

I'll also mention that the club had a notorious reputation for having a bigoted history. The Mayor quit the club following allegations it did not allow blacks or jews. The club had its first black representative in 1980. Jackie Robinson broke the color barrier in MLB 33 years prior. Giving them the award of my least favorite team in this list!
